# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to every project published by Cryoforge Nexus. It is maintained as a single shared document and mirrored into each repository, so the version here is identical to the one in every other Cryoforge Nexus project.

## The short version

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep the discussion on the work. Almost everyone reading this will never need the rest of the document.

## Standards

Open-source projects run on people volunteering their time and their reasoning in public. The rules below exist to keep that possible.

**Expected:**

- **Critique the work, not the person.** "This query runs once per member and will rate-limit" is useful. "This is amateur code" is not.
- **Assume competence and good faith.** Contributors come from different backgrounds, languages, and experience levels. A question that seems obvious to you was not obvious to whoever asked it.
- **Be explicit about the reason for a decision.** Closing an issue or rejecting a pull request without saying why is disrespectful of the time someone spent on it, even when the decision itself is correct.
- **Accept that maintainers decide scope.** A contribution can be well-written and still not belong in the project. That is a scope judgement, not a verdict on the contributor.
- **Keep discussion in the open.** Technical decisions belong in the issue or pull request they concern, where the reasoning stays available to everyone who reads it later.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained hostility toward anyone, in public or in private.
- Insults or attacks based on who someone is: gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, neurotype, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, nationality, age, religion, or any comparable characteristic.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or sexualized content in project spaces.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly reopening a settled decision to wear down the people involved.
- Continuing an interaction with someone after they have asked you to stop.
- Encouraging or coordinating any of the above.

Intent does not decide the outcome. A joke that lands as harassment is treated as harassment. What is judged is the effect on the person on the receiving end and the response once it is pointed out.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project occupies:

- The Forgejo instance at [git.cryoforgenexus.com](https://git.cryoforgenexus.com): issues, pull requests, code review, commit messages, and wikis.
- Public mirrors of these repositories.
- Official Cryoforge Nexus community spaces, including Discord servers and the hosted bot instances we operate.
- Any situation where someone is representing the project, such as speaking for it publicly or using an official account.

It also covers conduct **outside** these spaces when that conduct is directed at a member of this community and makes participating here unsafe for them. What happens elsewhere is not our business in general; harassment that follows someone back to our issue tracker is.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness a breach of this Code of Conduct, report it privately to **[conduct@cryoforgenexus.com](mailto:conduct@cryoforgenexus.com)**. The address reaches the Cryoforge Nexus maintainers and nobody else.

**Do not report a breach by opening a public issue.** Doing so exposes the person affected and turns the incident into a public argument, which is exactly what the reporting process exists to avoid.

A useful report includes what happened, where, roughly when, and links if the exchange is public. If you are unsure whether something crosses the line, report it anyway and let us make that call. Reporting in good faith carries no risk to your standing in the project, even if the conclusion is that nothing happened.

Reports are handled confidentially. Details are shared only with the people who need them to resolve the matter, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed to the reported person without the reporter's agreement.

## Enforcement

Cryoforge Nexus maintainers are responsible for enforcement and will investigate every report. Responses are chosen to fit the incident, and severe cases start further down the ladder rather than at the top of it:

1. **Private correction.** A private message explaining what was wrong and what is expected instead. Used when the behaviour looks like a misjudgement rather than a pattern.
2. **Warning.** A formal warning with defined conditions, such as no further interaction with the people involved for a set period. Breaking those conditions escalates.
3. **Temporary ban.** Removal from all project spaces for a defined period. Public or private contact with the people involved during that period is not permitted.
4. **Permanent ban.** Permanent removal from all project spaces.

Maintainers may also remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that breach this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when they do.

Maintainers are held to this document too. A maintainer who breaches it faces the same ladder, and reports about a maintainer should be sent through the same channel; where the report concerns the person who would normally handle it, another maintainer takes it over.

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement decision against you was wrong, you may appeal once by replying to [conduct@cryoforgenexus.com](mailto:conduct@cryoforgenexus.com) with your reasoning. Appeals are reviewed by a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision where one is available. The outcome of an appeal is final.
